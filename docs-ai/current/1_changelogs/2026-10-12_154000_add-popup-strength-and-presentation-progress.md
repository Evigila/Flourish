Time zone: America/Sao_Paulo (UTC-03:00). User authorization covers both Flourish and its Colligere consumer. Changes remain on master and are not committed or published.

SSR ActionMenu child commands receive the actual floating menu controller through the library's automatic input-behaviors entry point. Positioning anchors to the trigger rather than its large parent hover slot. Scoped theme roles transfer to the top-layer panel; no universal foreground correction overrides arbitrary host color choices.

PresentationBand and PresentationHero default to FullHeight=true and MinHeight=0. Their minimum is one 100dvh viewport and content can grow. PresentationFooter has natural height. At the footer the links occupy a dedicated normal-flow row after its content. SSR and enhanced navigation initialize through the library; observer/disposal cleanup preserves instance ownership. Explicit FullHeight=false/MinHeight and IncludeInProgress=false remain supported.
